Expiration Tracker

In Development

Track expiration dates on groceries, medications, and household items and get notified before they expire.

Android

About the App

Expiration Tracker helps you keep track of expiration dates for food, medicine, cosmetics, and household products so nothing goes to waste.

All data is stored locally on the device, so your inventory stays private and works fully offline without requiring a cloud account.

How to Use

1

Add an Item

Enter a product name, category, and expiration date manually or scan the barcode.

2

Set Your Alert

Choose how far in advance you want to be notified — one day, one week, or a custom window.

3

Track Your Inventory

View all tracked items in one list with color-coded status based on how soon they expire.

4

Get Notified

Receive local push reminders before items reach their expiration date.

Key Capabilities

Barcode Scanning

Add products quickly by scanning their barcode instead of typing.

Custom Alert Windows

Set per-item or global reminders days or weeks before expiration.

Color-Coded Status

Items are flagged as fresh, expiring soon, or expired at a glance.

Local Storage

Your inventory stays on-device with no cloud account required.
