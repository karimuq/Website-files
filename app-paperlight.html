A privacy-first document scanner that turns camera captures into clean, shareable PDFs.

About the App

Paperlight is a focused document-scanning app for capturing paper documents, improving their readability, and exporting them as polished PDFs. It is designed to keep the workflow quick and distraction-free.

Scans are processed locally on the device wherever possible, giving users a simple alternative to heavyweight scanning suites and cloud-first document tools.

How to Use

1

Capture a Document

Use the camera to photograph a receipt, note, form, or multi-page document.

2

Clean the Scan

Adjust the crop and apply a clear document filter for a sharper result.

3

Organize Pages

Review, reorder, and manage pages together in one document.

4

Export as PDF

Save or share a clean PDF directly from the app.

Key Capabilities

Document Enhancement

Improve contrast and readability with purpose-built scan filters.

Multi-Page PDFs

Combine multiple captured pages into a single shareable document.

Local Processing

Keep document content on the device instead of requiring a cloud upload.

Simple Export

Export finished documents quickly for sharing, storage, or printing.
